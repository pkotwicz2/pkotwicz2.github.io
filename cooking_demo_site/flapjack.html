Ole British Recipes

Flapjack

The classic British traybake: chewy, buttery oat bars sweetened with golden syrup. Makes 16 squares.

Timings

Total time
40 minutes
Baking time
25 minutes

Allow extra time for the flapjack to cool before cutting.

Ingredients

  • 250 g unsalted butter, plus extra for greasing
  • 250 g light brown soft sugar
  • 175 g golden syrup (about 3 tablespoons)
  • 425 g rolled oats (porridge oats)
  • A pinch of salt

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 180°C (160°C fan, gas mark 4). Grease and line a 20 × 30 cm baking tin with baking paper.
  2. Put the butter, sugar and golden syrup in a large pan. Heat gently, stirring, until the butter has melted and the sugar has dissolved.
  3. Take the pan off the heat and stir in the oats and salt until every oat is well coated.
  4. Tip the mixture into the tin and press it down firmly and evenly with the back of a spoon.
  5. Bake for 20 to 25 minutes, until golden around the edges but still slightly soft in the middle.
  6. Leave to cool in the tin for 10 minutes, then score into 16 squares with a knife. Leave to cool completely before cutting through and lifting out.

Cook’s tip

For a softer, chewier flapjack, bake for the shorter time. For a crunchier bar, bake a little longer. Stored in an airtight tin, it keeps for up to a week.
