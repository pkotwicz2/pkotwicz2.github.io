English Toffee Pudding

Also known as sticky toffee pudding: a soft, moist date sponge soaked in a rich toffee sauce. Serves 6.

Timings

Total time
1 hour 5 minutes
Baking time
35 minutes

Ingredients

For the sponge

  • 175 g pitted dates, chopped
  • 250 ml boiling water
  • 1 teaspoon bicarbonate of soda
  • 85 g unsalted butter, softened, plus extra for greasing
  • 140 g light muscovado sugar
  • 2 large eggs
  • 175 g self-raising flour
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract

For the toffee sauce

  • 100 g unsalted butter
  • 175 g dark muscovado sugar
  • 1 tablespoon black treacle
  • 225 ml double cream
  • A pinch of sea salt

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 180°C (160°C fan, gas mark 4). Grease a 20 cm square baking dish with butter.
  2. Put the dates in a bowl, pour over the boiling water and stir in the bicarbonate of soda. Leave to soak for 10 minutes.
  3. Beat the butter and light muscovado sugar together until pale and fluffy. Beat in the eggs one at a time, then the vanilla.
  4. Fold in the flour, then stir in the dates along with all of their soaking liquid. The batter will be quite runny.
  5. Pour into the prepared dish and bake for 30 to 35 minutes, until risen and springy, and a skewer pushed into the centre comes out clean.
  6. While the sponge bakes, make the sauce. Melt the butter, dark muscovado sugar and treacle in a pan over a low heat, stirring until the sugar dissolves.
  7. Stir in the cream and salt, bring to a gentle simmer and bubble for 3 minutes until glossy.
  8. Poke holes all over the warm sponge with a skewer and pour over a third of the sauce. Leave to soak for 5 minutes.
  9. Cut into squares and serve warm with the remaining sauce, plus custard, cream or vanilla ice cream.

Cook’s tip

The pudding can be made a day ahead. Reheat portions with a little sauce, covered, in a low oven or the microwave.
