Hot Cross Buns

Soft, lightly spiced buns full of dried fruit and citrus peel, traditionally eaten at Easter. Makes 12.

Timings

Total time
3 hours 30 minutes
Baking time
20 minutes

Total time includes about 2 hours 15 minutes of rising.

Ingredients

For the dough

  • 500 g strong white bread flour, plus extra for dusting
  • 75 g caster sugar
  • 7 g sachet fast-action dried yeast
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 2 teaspoons ground mixed spice
  • 1 teaspoon ground cinnamon
  • 300 ml whole milk
  • 50 g unsalted butter
  • 1 large egg, beaten
  • 150 g sultanas or raisins
  • 50 g mixed candied peel
  • Finely grated zest of 1 orange

For the crosses

  • 75 g plain flour
  • About 5 tablespoons water

For the glaze

  • 3 tablespoons apricot jam, or golden syrup
  • 1 tablespoon water

Method

  1. Warm the milk and butter in a pan until the butter has just melted. Leave to cool until lukewarm.
  2. In a large bowl, mix the bread flour, sugar, yeast, salt, mixed spice and cinnamon. Keep the salt and yeast on opposite sides of the bowl at first.
  3. Pour in the warm milk mixture and the beaten egg. Mix to a soft, slightly sticky dough.
  4. Knead on a lightly floured surface for 10 minutes, until smooth and stretchy.
  5. Put the dough in an oiled bowl, cover and leave somewhere warm for 1 to 1½ hours, until doubled in size.
  6. Knock back the dough and knead in the sultanas, mixed peel and orange zest until evenly spread.
  7. Divide into 12 equal pieces and roll each into a smooth ball. Place on a lined baking tray, leaving a little space between them.
  8. Cover loosely and leave to rise for another 45 minutes to 1 hour, until puffy. Meanwhile, heat the oven to 220°C (200°C fan, gas mark 7).
  9. Mix the plain flour with enough water to make a thick, pipeable paste. Spoon into a piping bag and pipe a cross over each bun.
  10. Bake for 18 to 20 minutes, until golden brown and hollow-sounding when tapped underneath.
  11. Warm the jam with the water, sieve if needed, and brush over the hot buns. Cool on a wire rack.

Cook’s tip

Hot cross buns are best eaten on the day, split and toasted with plenty of butter. They also freeze well for up to a month.
