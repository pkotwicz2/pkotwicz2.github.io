Apples: The Classic Orchard Fruit

Apples are crisp, sweet, and come in red, green, or yellow varieties. They are highly versatile and often used to make pies, sauce, or cider. They grow abundantly in temperate climates.

Oranges are juicy, tangy citrus fruits packed with Vitamin C. They are known for their bright, thick peels and are widely cultivated in subtropical and tropical climates for their juice and zest.
