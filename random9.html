Frequently asked questions

Only one answer can be open at a time. Opening a question closes the previously open one.

How do I reset my password?

Go to Settings > Account > Security and select Reset password. A reset link will be sent to your registered email address.

Can I change my username?

Yes. Usernames can be changed once every 30 days from the Profile page.

How do I delete my account?

Account deletion is permanent. Before deleting, download a copy of your data. Then follow these steps:

  1. Open Settings.
  2. Select Account.
  3. Choose Delete account and confirm.
Which browsers are supported?

The latest two versions of Chrome, Edge, Firefox, and Safari are supported.
